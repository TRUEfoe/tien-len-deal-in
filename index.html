Support & Contact

Need help? Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, feedback and anything else about the game go to one place, and a real person reads them:

truefoe@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few days. There is no support account to create and nothing to sign in to — just send an email.

Reporting a problem

Including these makes a bug far easier to track down:

  • Your device and iOS version — for example, “iPhone 14, iOS 18.2”.
  • The app version, shown on the App Store listing (currently 1.0.7).
  • Which mode you were in: Classic Mode or Roguelike Mode.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play Tiến Lên?

The game has a full walkthrough built in. Open How to Play from the main menu and it deals a real hand and talks you through it, a card at a time. You can leave it at any point with Skip.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your petals, relics, statistics and settings are saved on your device automatically. There is no account and nothing is stored on a server, which also means progress does not transfer between devices, and deleting the app deletes your progress with it.

Does the game work offline?

Yes — the whole game is offline. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Is real money involved anywhere?

No. Nothing in the game can be bought with real money, and nothing in it can be exchanged for real money or prizes. Everything you earn — lotus petals, and the luck a duel is played for — exists only inside the game.

What languages does it support?

English and Vietnamese. Switch between them at the bottom of the main menu, at any time.

How do I reset my relic collection?

Open Relics from the main menu and use the reset there. It locks every relic again and sets the unlock counters back to zero. It does not touch your petals, your banked Lucky Draws, or a run in progress — and it cannot be undone.

I found a rule that looks wrong.

Tiến Lên is played slightly differently from table to table, so this is worth reporting. Email us with the hand and what you expected, and we will take a look.
